# Revenant energy

Build **167409** | `game` | `s2c` | `0x01DC`

Set Revenant energy amount, maximum and regeneration rate during bootstrap or runtime updates.

Connection: World.

## Fields

| # | Field | Wire type | Constraints | Meaning |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `agent` | `varuint32` |  | Actor whose energy is updated. |
| 2 | `amount` | `f32` | float_policy=finite | Current energy amount. |
| 3 | `maximum` | `f32` | float_policy=finite | Maximum energy amount. |
| 4 | `rate` | `f32` | float_policy=finite | Energy regeneration rate. |

## Notes

- Energy updates use the same amount, maximum and regeneration-rate fields.
